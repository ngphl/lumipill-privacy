LumiPill Support

LumiPill is a medicine reminder app for iPhone, made by Phi Long Nguyen. It keeps a simple daily list of your medicines and reminds you at each dose time.

Contact us

Email hello.lumipill@gmail.com with any question, problem or idea. You do not need to send your medicine names or photos of your prescriptions; we never need your real medicines to fix a problem.

Reminders

  • A reminder did not arrive: open LumiPill once (it refreshes its reminders), and check that notifications are allowed for LumiPill in the iPhone's Settings, Notifications. A Focus can also hold reminders back.
  • To hide medicine names on the Lock Screen: in the iPhone's Settings, Notifications, Show Previews, choose When Unlocked.

Scanning a label or prescription

  • Scan results can be wrong. Compare every medicine and detail with the label or prescription before you save it.
  • Scanning needs the internet. Your photos leave your phone only when you tap Send photos.
  • If scanning is paused, or you have used this month's scans, you can still type a medicine in. Paused scanning starts again by itself.

Family sharing

  • Share your medicines with family, or stop sharing, in More, Family.

The Lock Screen countdown

  • Turn it on or off in More, Medicine reminders, Countdown on the Lock Screen.

Your information

  • Your medicine list stays on your phone, unless you share it with family, and is deleted with the app.
  • To ask about your information, or to have what our scanning service keeps deleted, email hello.lumipill@gmail.com.
  • The privacy policy: https://ngphl.github.io/lumipill-privacy/

Important

LumiPill is not medical advice. It only organises the schedule you confirm. Follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions, and ask them if you are unsure about a medicine. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
